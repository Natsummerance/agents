# GLOSSARY.md - blader/humanizer 术语词典

> 整理自 candidates/glossary.md，包含所有 skill 的共享术语

## 📚 核心术语

### Humanizer（去痕器）
**定义**: 移除文本中的 AI 生成痕迹，让文本读起来像人写的工具。

**原文引用**:
> "Humanizer rewrites AI-sounding text so it reads like a person wrote it, without changing what it says."

**相关技能**: 所有 skills

---

### 35 Patterns（35 个模式）
**定义**: 来自 Wikipedia 的"Signs of AI writing"模式列表，由 WikiProject AI Cleanup 维护。

**原文引用**:
> "Humanizer uses 35 patterns from Wikipedia's [\"Signs of AI writing\"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), maintained by WikiProject AI Cleanup."

**相关技能**: pattern-based-detection

---

### Voice Matching（声音匹配）
**定义**: 根据提供的写作样本匹配作者风格的机制。

**原文引用**:
> "If you provide a writing sample, Humanizer follows that sample instead of its default style rules."

**相关技能**: voice-matching

---

### First Rewrite + Critique（第一次重写 + 批评）
**定义**: 先进行第一次重写，然后对仍听起来人工的内容进行简短批评的流程。

**原文引用**:
> "When you paste text, Humanizer shows its work before giving you the final version. You see the first rewrite and a short critique of anything that still sounds artificial."

**相关技能**: two-pass-rewriting

---

### WikiProject AI Cleanup（AI 清理维基项目）
**定义**: 维护 Wikipedia"Signs of AI writing"模式列表的维基百科项目。

**原文引用**:
> "maintained by WikiProject AI Cleanup"

**相关技能**: pattern-based-detection
